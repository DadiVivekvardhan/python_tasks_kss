NEET paper leak

The Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) has filed a chargesheet against 13 accused in the NEET-UG paper leak case before the Rouse Avenue Court in Delhi.

The chargesheet has been submitted by the CBI's Economic Offences Wing. It will now be placed before the Special Fast Track Court set up to hear cases related to public examination paper leaks. The court will decide the next steps in the case.
